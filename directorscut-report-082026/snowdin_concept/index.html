VERY EARLY CONCEPTS

This is the first ever text document made on Director’s Cut. It didn’t even have a name yet… A lot of these ideas did end up making to the Demo, and some of the future ones mentioned in it will definitely happen… Probably.

Huh? "This isn't concept art"? ... I know it's not, but... I can't draw!!! Cut me some slack...

ENEMIES

Most of the enemies in the Demo were conceptualized by many different people in the team, like me, Mouse, Wild, Fanimator, etc., but ultimately realized by Hooded.

The first enemy we designed was Conerr, since it was one of Toby’s Hard Mode designs shown in the UNDERTALE Art Book. Our first attempt with the design was simply making the original sketch on the Artbook ‘cooler’.

Back then I thought it still needed to look a little ‘cute’, though. One more attempt later, the final design ended up coming up as a joke. Hooded posted this in the group chat we had for the project with the caption “you only wanteth me for my beauty”... I initially was kinda confused but I quickly warmed up to it. Conerr also ended up being the first enemy we sprited and added into the game!

Thus ends the historieth of thy fair maiden…

The second enemy we designed was Dragonflake. I remember I came up with the main ideas for his design, my biggest inspiration being Chinese dragons, because of some notes present in Magnolia Porter’s original sketch for Snowdrake (e.g calling it a ‘snow dragon’). Dragon’s wood-y arms were also inspired by that sketch.

Hooded’s first sketch included glasses and a tie, which, embarrassing as it is to admit, was only noticed by me when writing this section… Oops. According to Hooded, it was meant to give the impression of a snob, or a critic. In the end, though, he just ended up as a normal teen who hates jokes…

The second sketch is closer to the design used in game, although I do think we underused the more hand-y elements in it. Oh well…

This is one of my favorite enemies from Snowdin.
Mouse was the one to suggest the krampus inspiration, as well as the candle horns (hanukkah, the jewish holiday). In turn, I was the one to suggest the fact that it steals gifts (and the lighthearted return of them), as well as it standing on two feet.

Its story is that teenagers decorating it got punished by being stolen from. Snowdin Town felt bad, and gave them gifts to compensate. Now it’s a tradition. This is actually mentioned in game by that blue bear NPC in front of the Christmas tree in the town.

Oh, this guy… Wyrmflake. He’s a little obscure, as he’s Chilldrake’s harder version. The idea of a rebellious teen becoming a hippie was pretty funny in my head. Its name derives from “Wyrm” as a form of dragon, but also its meaning of someone naive and gullible. Then, Wild came up with its actual design, as seen in the first sketch. Then Hooded drew his version based off the original concept and our ideas, and it stuck. Story of Wyrmflake.

THE HONORED ONE

“A full, complete hard mode for Undertale is something that a lot of people, including myself have wanted for a while. Thankfully, Director’s Cut does just that. One of the most important and looked-toward things about Hard Mode are the bullet patterns, and I like creating them. I joined around the time that Snowdin was somewhat close to being done. So as a result most of the attacks I made in the released demo was for Papyrus’ battle. Here’s some of the concepts for Papyrus’ attacks (with my amazing and totally professional drawings)!”

- The Honored One

MOUSE

“Joining this project was sorta something I did on a whim, I never really expected to get anywhere but I ended up being a way bigger part of this project than I thought I would be, from sprites, to concept art, to writing and to bullet patterns, I have done quite a lot to this project in hindsight…and honestly it’s been so surreal, I never thought I would ever contribute to something like this.”

- Mouse

“I sorta hate spriting long wiggly things, so Dragonflake’s body went through many changes before it landed on what we got [...], however all that work did turn into the beautiful sprite we have today. So I’m proud of myself!”

“I also worked on some minor stuff like Trouble and Boys, or as they’re more commonly known…. Troubleboys. [...] Plus, I made that jumping attack we see in game! It was very fun to see it come to light once the coders got a hang of things!“

“[...] Making concepts for some monster attacks (Conerr, Gifttheft, Toriel, Troubleboys) has always been fun and honestly I think I have a knack for coming up with bullet patterns.”

“Speaking of [...] early on, I actually would code attacks into CYF to show off what I meant. Though I did sorta abandon this as it means I would have to make the sprites myself, cause I hate using placeholders and like half the time I can’t even do it justice.”

“Lastly, ever since I joined I have been drawing more, for attacks, or concepts with scenes, or monsters… And it has led to a few of my designs getting sprites and me getting better at drawing, and while I’m still not perfect, I hope that my work continues to give you all a wonderful experience when the time comes for the next demo! Just remember if you sit in things forever you’ll never go anywhere, even if you get rejected you’ll at least have tried!”

“So, dream big! Slay a dragon! Shine brighter than the sun! Work towards what feels like you! And never, ever give up your hopes and dreams. I hope that just like UNDERTALE inspired me, whoever is reading this will be inspired by my work too!”
