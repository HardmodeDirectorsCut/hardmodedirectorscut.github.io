PROGRESS REPORT

REPORT 2 (Summer 2026)

Hello... J here...

It’s… really… HOT! (Well, not really. It’s winter where I live.) Who even LIKES summer?! Huh? You do? Uhhh. Well, that’s only because you have a pool to chill in, I bet! Yeah!

Err, pesky summer aside…

Progress hasn’t been as speedy as it was back at the start of the year, but we still did a lot in these 6 months that passed! (Technically 5, at the time I’m writing this…)

Well, let’s jump into the juicy details now, shall we?

First of all, you might have noticed, but this Progress Report marks the debut of our website!

It was a big collaborative effort between Miles, shortpie and Oktav. I also did some small stuff… But all of the hard work was theirs!

So you should check out their stuff and support them, they deserve it!

Like I mentioned previously, progress grew a little slower over the recent months, mostly due to many people of our team (especially our programmers) being busy. Even me! To counter that, we hired a few new faces. In the programming department we have: Nyx and Anti Guy! Both of them are working hard, and we’ll get into more details later in this report! Now, in the music department, one of our previous musicians, Virtual, was promoted to the director of the department, so, in turn, a new musician was invited to the team, that being Kroomba! He’s made a very important and quite awaited song from later in the game pretty much right off the bat. I hope to see your thoughts on that in the future…

Overall, we’ve focused on getting all of the random encounters finished, while we planned for the mini bosses and Undyne. As of now, pretty much all random encounters are finished, only missing some bullet patterns (specifically group ones). We are currently in the process of wrapping up that and moving slowly but surely to the bigger encounters.

With that in mind, here’s a little bit of what we’ve been working on…

Meet Splashdon and Zzteve! The harder versions of Woshua and Aaron. Both of them were really fun to write and plan attacks for!

Splashdon is a funny little creature. Machine? Umm… It wants to hydrate the entire world! It also comes with a drinking goose. In turn, Zzteve’s a total hunk! Like, have you seen that 20-pack? He is a little arrogant, though… And he may or may not burn you to crisp with a lighting strike.

In other aspects, planning for Waterfall has been wrapped, meaning we know what will happen and what to do from head to toe. Now we just need to add it in the game (hard)...

Oh, we’ve also gotten a lot of overworld sections done. There may be a little more to Waterfall than what first meets the eye now…

Overall:

  • PLANNING: 100%
  • WRITING: 100%
  • ENEMY PROGRAMMING: 50%
  • BULLET PATTERNS: 45%
  • ASSETS: 90%
  • MUSIC: 95%

    • I’d say we are 12% out of 16% complete with Waterfall, meaning we are 44% done with the full game. We are approaching the 50% mark rapidly…!!!

    Ah, right, something about what Nyx and Anti each worked on?

    This one isn’t new for those who watched the 1st Anniversary Stream, but Nyx added the fabled console borders into the game! They’re very pleasant to look at while testing, and they also make sense considering the other console-exclusive shrines were already in the game since v1.0! You’ll only see these beauties in action when v2.0 releases, though…

    A first look at Mad Dummy! Well, the design is pretty much the exact same as the RUINS Dummy, but, see, it has cool teeth!!! The sprites seen were done by Milan, and the programming was a collaborative effort between Anti and Real, but the former is getting to work on the bullet patterns as of now.

    Speaking of her, I’d like to touch upon a few of our plans for this first battle, and also the Mad Mew Mew battle!

    Overall, we plan to have new dummy bullet types that will be used, along with a few wacky and fun ideas for the bullet patterns in general. The ending to the battle will also be a bit different…

    As for Mad Mew Mew, we’ve commented before we plan to make her battle harder. Um, I’ll be honest, we’re not so sure on what to do yet. But it’ll begin brewing someday soon. She’s only coming in the final version of the game, after all. … Sorry, Mad Mew Mewers!

    (PS: Thanks to DELTARUNE Chapter 5, we already have SOME ideas brewing for her… But you’ll have to wait and see!)

    It has been roughly eight months since the Snowdin Demo released, and so, I thought it would be interesting to show off the many concepts we made before making them reality in the game! Enjoy…

    Continuing our last progress report, we come back to the hard versions of Temmies — Tuyokis, and their village, which you can see in the video!
    Since the last time we mentioned them, we've successfully finished the village and shop themes, and we also got around to spriting and programming the shop. So, it's pretty much finished? Umm, it feels weird to dedicate a section to it when there's not much left to say...

    Oh, and I thought it'd also be neat to go ahead and release both the village and shop themes already. Enjoy...?



    Anyway, all the Tuyokis have wacky dialogues, especially the shop one! I can't wait for you to experience it in-game.

    When it comes to the future, we already have many things planned for the later areas of the game, though very surface-level, like enemies and their designs, plus a few sparse ideas for puzzles. We usually wait until we get into working on that area to plan more in depth.

    That is to say, development is starting for Hotland! With many departments wrapping up their work on Waterfall, we’re already moving our part of the team to work on the former. At the time of writing this, only the writing and art department have been assigned to Hotland tasks.

    However, we can't talk too much about our future plans without spoiling the mod. So let’s look at the past for a bit, shall we?

    Because we keep the public demo and in-development demo branches separate in the source code, this allows us to transfer bug fixes, features and more between versions! And we've recently noted how similar the bullet patterns for some of the hard-mode exclusive monsters in Ruins are that haven't had a CORE equivalent to swap with. So, relatively soon we might make a public patch changing that, as well as a few minor things!


    Well, that's about everything we had to show off. While we've already talked about the progress, but if we had to say one last time... right now we're in a in-between state where we already finished more than half of Waterfall including enemies, and are also going to distribute the first test builds of Waterfall for testers soon enough. And yet, there is still a lot to finish, like the boss fight at the end for every route...

    If there's one thing we can confirm about the release date, it's the fact it won't be this Summer! Overall, we’ve had good progress, yet we still need to finish the mini bosses, the bosses and test it enough that there are no bugs or crashes.

    Well,

    Thank you for reading!

    Enjoy the rest of your morning!
