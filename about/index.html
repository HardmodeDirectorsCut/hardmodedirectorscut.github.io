... is a mod that aims to complete
UNDERTALE's unfinished Hard Mode!

It intends to remain somewhat faithful to Toby's vision,
but also with lots of new flavor from the team, the Directors Crew.

Things have changed in this world since you
last saw it.

Everyone has gotten stronger, and the world
became hellish to any humans who dare enter!

Though, it seems that a lot of this is due to a
"memo" every monster has received.

Despite this, each enemy can still be "defeated" nonviolently.

Fix faces! Build snow statues! Repair broken marriages!

Or, ignore this choice and rain destruction
upon your foes! After all, they attacked
you first.

Your journey will take
determination. A lot of it!

As each adversary's bullet patterns
have gotten more difficult.

(Don't worry, some are real hard!!!)

This mod also has lots more features,
such as:

  • * Many additions to the RUINS, such as harder puzzles, overworld sprites for Final Froggit and Parsnik, and more.

  • * Brand new equipment, with special features within them!

  • * Hellishly difficult hidden bosses!

  • * Brand new cutscene dialogue and flavor text, all while staying true to Toby Fox's signature humor.

  • * All-new Trophies, alongside the existing PS4 ones!

  • * An Extras menu with helpful settings, such as: Trophy viewing, save data management, etc.
