FAQ!

last updated June 2 2026

I encountered an issue!
First, check the Troubleshooting section a bit lower in this page. If your issue is not mentioned there or you were not able to solve it, please contact us at uthardmode.directorscut@gmail.com! Make sure to explain how the issue happened, and send the “crash.log” file, which you can find in the mod’s save file folder (go to %localappdata% and locate the folder called “HardMode”.)
Do you still need to name yourself "Frisk" to activate Hard Mode?
Yes, you still do.
What are the release plans for this mod?
We plan on releasing various demos, each containing another area of the original game. So:
  • Demo v1.0 - RUINS & Snowdin Demo
  • Demo v2.0 - Waterfall Demo
  • Demo v3.0 - Hotland & CORE Demo
  • Full Game!
    • Will this mod have an Android port?
    Likely not. Since it's a mod, it'd be hard to port and it'd also require you to obtain an 'illegal' port of UNDERTALE.
    (Though, if you find a way to make a port work... we wouldn't mind. Just be careful with how you distribute it, and don't say it was made by us!)
    Will the story be changed in any major way?
    No, it will not. However, many scenes (especially puzzle-related ones) will have differences here and there.
    Will [Character] get a new design?
    It depends! All random enemies and most mini bosses will get new designs, whereas main characters will not. Secret bosses like Glyde and So Sorry won't either, though Mad Mew Mew will.
    Will characters like Asgore, Toriel, Papyrus, etc, get genocide battles?
    No. Most of the characters that did not have fights in the genocide route fights in Normal Mode will also not have them here. Mostly because it does not fit their character. We do not plan on changing that any time soon. As such, do not insist...
    Will Mettaton NEO have a battle?
    Yes!! He will have one!!!
    It has been awhile since the last Update. Is this cancelled?
    Not at all! We do not plan on cancelling any time soon. If we end up not giving updates, it either means we just don't have anything interesting or spoiler-free to show. Apologies if that is the case!

    TROUBLESHOOTING

    I am not able to patch the mod. A message says it's the "wrong file"!
    Make sure you have the Steam version. If you don’t, simply download “v108-win-drmfree.xdelta” and apply it to your UNDERTALE’s “data.win” like you’d apply any mod patch.
    A "Select File" window appears when running the mod!
    Make sure all of the files are in the same folder as the game's runner.
    The text is missing/blank!
    You most likely forgot to drag and drop or deleted the “lang” folder. Acquire it and place it back on the game’s folder.
    The music suddenly cuts!
    That is a known issue. However, at the time of writing this, we have no idea what causes this. Believe me, I know how annoying and disruptive this can be, but as of now there is nothing we can do. I’m sorry.
    I got trapped in the collisions/invisible walls!
    Please report to us where you got stuck, and we’ll fix it as soon as we can. Sorry about the inconvenience.
